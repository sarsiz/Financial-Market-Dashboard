Rates interpretation

Facts · interpretation · watch next
FACT

What changed?

India bond market is seeing higher yields with flat curve signals — 2Y 6.92% (−1bp), 10Y 7.09% (+2bp), 30Y 7.23% (+2bp).

INTERPRETATION

Why it changed

The dominant read is inflation expectations, reinforced by inflation-driven Reserve Bank of India expectations. The curve is bear-flattening — long-end leading.

IMPLICATION

What it implies

Banks, domestic cyclicals and pricing-power names lead the equity read-through. RBI stance is restrictive — watching food inflation, liquidity and crude pass-through. Flat curves matter most for banks and duration-heavy sectors.

PROTOCOL

Research protocol

Anchor on a flat curve · inflation-driven read · explicit policy / event transmission. Layer interpretation only after facts, cadence and provenance are established.

VERY HIGH

Government bond curve

Track 2Y, 5Y, 10Y, 30Y levels and 2s10s / 5s30s slope before drawing any equity conclusion. intraday · FRED / local bond cache

VERY HIGH

Inflation expectations

Track CPI, core CPI, breakevens and real policy gap together. daily / release days · BLS / CPI references / local cache

VERY HIGH

Central bank policy path

Use central-bank calendar, current policy rate and bias language before inferring a regime shift. event-driven · Fed / RBI calendar

NOTE

Regime note

Rising front-end yields usually signal tighter policy expectations or stickier inflation. Bull steepening often points to easing expectations or recession concern. Bear steepening usually points to higher term premium, supply or stronger growth/inflation repricing.
